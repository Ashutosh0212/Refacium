Safety Intelligence

Facial Risk Heatmaps —
Refacium’s Key Differentiator

Unlike sonography (reactive & technical), Refacium predicts risk before any injection. Unlike Crisalix or Vectra (aesthetics only), Refacium ensures beauty and safety at every step.

Red — High Risk

Inner eye corners, glabella, nasal bridge. Risks include blindness, necrosis, and stroke. Requires maximum caution and a specialist approach.

  • Glabella & forehead centre
  • Nasal bridge & dorsum
  • Medial canthus (inner eye corners)
  • Nasolabial fold — medial segment

Yellow — Moderate Risk

Safe with correct technique. Cannula preferred over needle. Misplacement risks swelling, vascular occlusion, and embolism.

  • Cheeks & malar eminence
  • Lip borders & vermilion
  • Perioral region
  • Lower eyelid / tear trough

Green — Safer Zones

Fewer vascular structures. Safer for contouring and augmentation with standard technique and appropriate depth.

  • Jawline & mandibular border
  • Lateral forehead & temples
  • Chin & mentum
  • Lateral cheek & pre-auricular

Surgical Roadmap Included: For every case, Refacium generates a step-by-step procedure plan with patient safety checks, suggested tool type (needle vs cannula), and safe filler depth ranges — so surgeons can explain the entire plan to patients with confidence.
